# HPP & Operational Dashboard

A lightweight business dashboard designed to monitor production costs, pricing, and operational performance through a Google Sheets–style workflow.

## Overview
This project was built as a portfolio-ready demo to showcase the ability to turn operational business needs into a functional digital tool. The app focuses on HPP, COGS, BOP, ROI, cash flow, working capital, capex, margin, and food cost analysis.

## Key Features
- Product and cost input table
- HPP calculation per product
- COGS and BOP analysis
- ROI and margin tracking
- Food cost visibility
- Cash flow and working capital overview
- Capex monitoring
- CSV import/export for Google Sheet-style data handling
- Responsive dashboard layout for business reporting

## Tech Stack
- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- CSV import/export workflow

## Why it matters
The goal of this project is to demonstrate a practical business solution: turning raw operational data into readable insight for better decision-making. It reflects the intersection of business logic, operational thinking, and user-friendly digital design.

Open the static app locally or use the included demo data to simulate a real production dashboard.
